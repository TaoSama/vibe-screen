The raw local adb serial is redacted to <redacted-adb-serial> in retained logs.
Absolute workspace paths in UTP and Gradle logs are redacted to <workspace>.
Retained artifacts are XML, textproto, UTP log, instrumentation status log, Gradle runner log, and text metadata. Omitted artifacts include binary pb files, cpuinfo, meminfo, lock files, and raw per-test logcat files.
